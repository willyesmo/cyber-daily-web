Right now, our team is reviewing your security team’s report on defenses against ransomware attacks. However, it appears you haven’t adequately protected yourselves. We gained access through the initial entry point and spent two days inside, where we obtained ESX administrator privileges. After some time, you took the ESX device offline, and we thought we’d been detected. But when we checked again the next day, it seemed you hadn’t realized you’d been attacked—our access credentials hadn’t been changed, and you’d even brought the ESX device back online. We then continued our attack on your domain controllers: samsung-cps, samsung-rtm, and samsung-brn. What really surprised us was that all your passwords were set to “Samsung01!” We then began downloading 1 TB of data. Over the weekend, we encrypted your systems. A few hours after we confirmed that most of the data had been encrypted, your security team finally detected the breach, took all machines offline, and changed all access credentials. What struck us was that we remained undetected for over a month, yet your security team only reacted so quickly once the encryption had already taken place. Your security team failed to adequately protect your company against this ransomware attack.
